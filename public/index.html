Meet Satya, Founder & Mathematics Educator

I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics and bring more than 15 years of teaching experience to Satya Academy. I’m a former Assistant Professor, a top-rated mentor at CodeYoung, and a Cuemath-certified teacher.

My teaching combines strong subject knowledge with clear explanations, carefully sequenced examples, and a supportive learning environment. I help students build confidence from middle-school mathematics through advanced university topics, while preparing them for demanding AP and SAT examinations.

Students I have guided in AP courses have earned scores of 4–5, and my SAT Math students have achieved scores in the 780–800 range. These are examples of student outcomes, not guaranteed results; individual progress depends on each learner’s preparation and effort.
