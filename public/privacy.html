Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Satya Academy respects the privacy of students and families. This page explains the information collected when you submit a free-trial lesson request.

Information you provide

The trial request form may collect a parent or adult student's name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the student's first name, grade or level, subject, curriculum, preferred date and time window, time zone, and learning goals or notes. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

How the information is used

The information is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss learning requirements, and arrange or confirm a trial lesson. Submitting the form is a request only; it does not create a confirmed appointment or enrolment.

Who can access it

Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify as the website hosting and form-processing provider, and are accessible to authorised Satya Academy personnel for handling the enquiry. Do not submit information about a child unless you are their parent or legal guardian, or otherwise authorised to do so.

Retention and requests

Information is retained only as reasonably needed to respond to the enquiry, maintain relevant business records, or meet legal obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion of an enquiry, contact satya@satyaacademy.org.
